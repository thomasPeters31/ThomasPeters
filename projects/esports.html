Esports Website — Yokai

A modern, competitive website for an esports team, built to establish a strong team identity online. Features bold visuals, team profiles, and a match schedule, with the site currently live and in active development.

View repo Visit live site Back to projects

Project snapshot

Role: Designer + Developer

Focus: Branding, team profiles, match updates

Status: In development

Goals and approach

The site leans into a high-contrast esports aesthetic while keeping typography and content structure readable. Every section is designed to spotlight roster details, team achievements, and match schedules without overwhelming visitors — balancing energy with clarity.

Team profiles

Individual player cards with roles, stats, and social links to give the roster a strong online presence.

Match updates

Dedicated section for upcoming fixtures and recent results, kept easy to update as the season progresses.

Visual identity

Bold gradients, strong imagery, and clear visual hierarchy designed to match the competitive tone of the team.

Tech stack

HTML CSS JavaScript UI/UX

Outcome

The site is live with core pages in place. Active development continues on the match schedule module, team media integration, and a performance pass ahead of a full public launch.
