J9Education

Currently in development, this website is for a maths tutor and career fair organiser. The design prioritises simplicity and trust, helping prospective students and parents find the right information quickly and feel confident making contact.

View repo Visit live site Back to projects

Project snapshot

Role
Designer + Developer
Focus
Client website, tutoring services, career fairs
Status
In development

Goals and approach

The client needed a professional, but simple site that clearly communicates their maths tutoring offer and their upcoming career fairs. The design prioritises simplicity and trust — prospective students and parents need to find the right information quickly and feel confident making contact.

Tutoring services

Clear presentation of subjects, levels covered, and how sessions work — keeping it straightforward for parents and students.

Career fairs

Dedicated section highlighting the client's career fair work, giving that side of their offering equal visibility alongside tutoring.

Client work

Worked closely with the client to understand their audience and translate their requirements into a clean, usable design.

Tech stack

HTML CSS JavaScript

Outcome

An in-progress client site that demonstrates the ability to translate a real brief into a clean, purposeful web presence. The project adds further real-world client experience alongside CounsellingHQ, covering a different audience and content structure.
